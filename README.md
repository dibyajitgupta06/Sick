# Home, Together — prototype

A small browser-playable cozy life-sim prototype. The full apartment stays visible while you walk between rooms, complete care tasks, escort her to the attached ensuite, bring a meal, and finish with a cuddle.

## Play

Play the prototype at [dibyajitgupta06.github.io/Sick](https://dibyajitgupta06.github.io/Sick/). Use **WASD** or the arrow keys to walk, then **E** to interact. You can also tap the glowing map markers and the on-screen movement pad.

The scene includes the bedroom and attached ensuite, lounge, dining area, kitchen, balcony, and main entrance. The TV automatically plays Billie Eilish’s “Ocean Eyes” when you turn it on. After all care tasks are done, the **Cuddle & sleep** button appears. After taking her to the ensuite, use **Carry her back to bed** to bring her back yourself.
